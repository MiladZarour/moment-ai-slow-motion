# Moment

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Choose or drop a local video, press Play, and hold the bottom-right circle (or the S key) for slow motion. Release to restore normal speed. Holding while paused previews in slow motion and pauses again when released.

Choose ⅛×, ¼×, or ½× playback. Includes seeking, mute, fullscreen, touch support, and keyboard access. Videos stay on your device; the optional Google Fonts stylesheet is fetched online, with local font fallbacks offline.

This is a playback tool. It does not export a modified video or generate interpolated frames. Smoothness depends on the source frame rate and browser codec support. MP4 with H.264 and WebM are good starting points.
